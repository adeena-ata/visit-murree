Essential Safety & Travel Tips

  • Check Weather Forecasts: Verify Pakistan Meteorological Department (PMD) alerts before starting your journey, especially between December and February.
  • Winter Vehicle Preparation: Ensure your car battery, heater, brakes, and tyres are in good condition, and carry snow chains during snowfall alerts.
  • Fuel & Supplies: Fill your fuel tank in Islamabad or Rawalpindi before climbing the hills, and pack warm clothes, water, and basic medicines.
  • Follow Traffic Rules: Avoid parking on narrow mountain turns and follow instructions from Murree Traffic Police and Rescue 1122 officials.
  • Best Time to Start: Depart early in the morning to avoid weekend traffic congestion near Lower Topa and Mall Road.
