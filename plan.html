Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the best time of year to visit Murree?

April to October offers pleasant green weather for families and hikers, while late December to February is best for experiencing snowfall.

2. How long does it take to reach Murree from Islamabad?

Via the Murree Expressway (E-75), the 60 km drive usually takes between 1.5 and 2 hours depending on traffic and weather conditions.

3. Is Murree safe for student group trips?

Yes, Murree is a popular destination for university trips. Stick to main tourist spots, check weather advisories in advance, and keep emergency contacts handy.
