About Murree

History & Location

Murree is a famous mountain resort town located in the Galyat region of the Pir Panjal Range, approximately 60 km northeast of Islamabad at an altitude of 2,291 metres (7,516 feet).

Founded in 1851 as a summer sanatorium during the British era, Murree is fondly known as the "Queen of the Hills" and welcomes hundreds of thousands of domestic and international tourists every year.

Seasons & Weather

  • Spring (March – May): Pleasant temperatures (12°C to 22°C) with blooming wildflowers and clear skies.
  • Summer (June – August): Cool escape from the plains (15°C to 25°C) with refreshing monsoon showers.
  • Autumn (September – November): Crisp mountain air, golden pine leaves, and peaceful sightseeing.
  • Winter (December – February): Heavy snowfall (-5°C to 8°C); ideal for snow lovers with proper road precautions.
